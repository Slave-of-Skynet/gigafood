## 1. Executive Commercial Summary

The economic and procurement narrative for GigaFood / PackShift is built on complete transparency:

1. **We did not rely on theoretical global materials:** We conducted rigorous commercial research to identify what can actually be purchased, quoted, or delivered in Romania today.
2. **The local market benchmark:** A conventional Romanian rotisserie market bag (Barleta 128002 paper + PP, B3) costs **0.37026 RON incl. VAT**.
3. **The local aluminium option is an expensive technical fallback:** Sourcing the locally available aluminium portion pair (C6-RO-P: E-ambalaj 729 tray + La Habibi clear lid) costs **1.1485–1.2915 RON incl. VAT**, representing a **+210.2% to +248.8% premium** over the B3 conventional market bag.
4. **Profi incumbent cost is confidential and UNKNOWN:** We cannot claim our solution costs "+X% vs Profi today" because Profi's actual purchase price is unmeasured.
5. **The mentor's +10–15% premium:** A ~10–15% cost premium was identified as a realistic business design target for a genuinely sustainable solution, not an automatic procurement approval threshold. For C6-RO-P to fit within a 15% ceiling, Profi's current bag would need to cost at least 1.00–1.12 RON, which is implausible for lightweight plastic bags. Therefore, preferred commercial development must focus on our renewable fibre candidates (C1 Gaia and C5 BIOPAP), where factory RFQs must establish direct commercial pricing.

## 3. Deep Dive: Locally Sourced Aluminium Option (C6-RO-P)

To provide Profi with a concrete, testable physical sample path, we modelled an order of 1,400 paired aluminium portion containers from two active Romanian web merchants:

### Component A: Tray Body
- **Seller:** E-ambalaj ([www.e-ambalaj.ro](https://www.e-ambalaj.ro/produs/caserole-necompartimentate-din-aluminiu-729/))
- **Product:** Caserole necompartimentate din aluminiu 729 (SKU `e-7291`)
- **Dimensions / Volume:** 220 × 170 × 35 mm • 1,125 ml capacity
- **Pricing:**
  - Promotional price: 76.00 RON / pack of 100 buc (incl. 19% VAT) = **0.7600 RON / unit**
  - Standard reference price: 90.30 RON / pack of 100 buc (incl. 19% VAT) = **0.9030 RON / unit**
- **Delivery Terms:** Free shipping on orders over 500 RON (S23); 14 packs (1,400 units) = 1,064.00 RON (exceeds threshold). Estimated delivery: 4–5 business days.

### Component B: Transparent Lid
- **Seller:** La Habibi ([lahabibi.ro](https://lahabibi.ro/caserole-aluminiu/4296-capac-caserole-680681729-100bucset.html))
- **Product:** Capac caserole 680681/729 (SKU `a-680681`)
- **Material:** Transparent plastic (exact polymer unstated)
- **Pricing:** 38.85 RON / pack of 100 buc (incl. 19% VAT) = **0.3885 RON / unit**
- **Delivery Terms:** Free shipping on orders over 500 RON; 14 packs (1,400 units) = 543.90 RON (exceeds threshold). Estimated delivery: 24–48 hours.

### Complete Paired Unit Economics
$$\text{Unit Cost (Low/Promotional)} = 0.7600 + 0.3885 = 1.1485 \text{ RON incl. VAT}$$
$$\text{Unit Cost (Standard)} = 0.9030 + 0.3885 = 1.2915 \text{ RON incl. VAT}$$

### Commercial Limitations & Caveats
1. **Cross-seller fit assumed:** Although both products cite the industry-standard "729" form factor, physical sealing and snap-fit between different manufacturers must be verified with physical samples.
2. **Order unit vs industrial MOQ:** 100-pack web pricing reflects small-batch commercial ordering, not volume procurement contracts.
3. **Closure thermal limits:** Transparent lid material and thermal resistance are unpublished; lid cannot enter a 250°C oven and must be fitted post-cooking at hot-hold temperatures.

## 4. Cost Comparison: C6-RO-P vs Market Reference (B3)

We compare the locally sourceable aluminium pair against the conventional Romanian rotisserie market benchmark (Barleta 128002 paper + PP bag, 0.37026 RON incl. VAT):

$$\Delta_{\text{low}} = \frac{1.1485 - 0.37026}{0.37026} \times 100\% = +210.2\%$$
$$\Delta_{\text{high}} = \frac{1.2915 - 0.37026}{0.37026} \times 100\% = +248.8\%$$

```text
Conventional Romanian Rotisserie Bag (B3):  ████ 0.37 RON
C6-RO-P Aluminium Pair (Low / Promo):       ████████████ 1.15 RON  (+210.2%)
C6-RO-P Aluminium Pair (Standard):          █████████████ 1.29 RON (+248.8%)
Mentor Cost Target (+15% ceiling over B3):  █████ 0.43 RON
```

### Strategic Interpretation for Profi
- **This is not good economic news:** A packaging cost of 1.15–1.29 RON represents a major increase over conventional rotisserie bags (~0.37 RON).
- **Why we disclose this openly:** Presenting aluminium as a cheap, ready-to-go green replacement would be dishonest. We present it as a **bounded technical high-temperature fallback**, demonstrating that our system models financial reality alongside technical constraints.
- **The +10–15% mentor target:** If Profi's packaging cost tolerance is +10–15%, a high-cost aluminium container cannot be the primary commercial solution unless offset by premium deli pricing or food-waste reductions.

## 6. Actionable RFQ Roadmap for Profi Procurement

To transition from hackathon research to formal procurement, we provide Profi with pre-drafted RFQ specifications targeting the 3 primary candidate leads:

```text
Phase 1: Operational Alignment (Week 1)
  │  ├── Confirm deli hot-fill temperature (e.g. 70°C vs 85°C)
  │  ├── Confirm maximum cabinet hold duration (e.g. 4h vs 6h)
  │  └── Weigh 10 empty incumbent Profi bags to establish true baseline
  │
Phase 2: RFQ Dispatch (Weeks 2–3)
  │  ├── Sacma SpA: Request Gaia P1 rotisserie bag sample, layer GSM, NatureFlex window TDS, delivered quote
  │  ├── BIOPAP Srl: Request LC SI-14 tray sample, resolve 175°C vs 185°C conflict, nominate barrier film SKU
  │  └── E-ambalaj / La Habibi: Order 100 sample pairs of 729 body + clear lid to test physical snap-fit
  │
Phase 3: Lab Testing & Quote Reconciliation (Weeks 4–6)
  │  ├── Migration testing (EN 1186 / EN 13130) with hot poultry fat
  │  ├── 6-hour warming cabinet holding test (steam, grease barrier, anti-fog clarity)
  │  └── Compare formal delivered quotes against the +10–15% budget tolerance
  │
Phase 4: Store Pilot & System Scale (Weeks 7–10)
     ├── 2-store customer trial for P1 (Whole Chicken) and P2 (Wings/Thighs)
     └── Input verified laboratory and pricing data into PackShift engine
```

### Specific Supplier RFQ Checklists

#### For Sacma SpA (C1 Gaia):
1. Nominate specific B.Life Gaia article code for whole rotisserie chicken (approx. 1.2–1.5 kg bird).
2. Disclose complete layer specification: kraft paper GSM, adhesive chemistry, NatureFlex film gauge.
3. Supply food-contact Declaration of Compliance (DoC) for fatty foods at elevated temperatures (>80°C).
4. Provide published anti-fog performance data for the NatureFlex transparent window under hot condensation.
5. Provide delivered price quotation for Romania at 25,000, 50,000, and 100,000 unit tiers.

#### For BIOPAP Srl (C5 LC SI-14):
1. Provide written clarification resolving the peak oven temperature conflict: 175°C / 60 min vs 185°C / 60 min.
2. Nominate the exact transparent heat-sealing film SKU and roll width compatible with SI-14 trays and standard tray sealers.
3. Supply complete DoC and overall/specific migration test results under hot-hold conditions (e.g. 90°C for 6 hours with food simulant D2 / vegetable oil).
4. Confirm current Romanian distribution channel (verify status of 2021 partner ReVive or direct export terms).
5. Supply delivered price quotation for SI-14 trays (per pallet/case) and compatible sealing film (per linear meter).
