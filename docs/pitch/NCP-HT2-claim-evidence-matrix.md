## 1. Epistemic Classification Scheme & Authority Hierarchy

Every factual assertion, slide statement, spoken pitch phrase, demo action, and judge Q&A answer is mapped to one of the following epistemic classifications. Lower levels never silently override higher levels:

1. `OFFICIAL_REQUIREMENT`: Explicitly stated in the official AgriFood Hackathon challenge rules/brief.
2. `MENTOR_CLARIFICATION`: Attributed to the Profi mentor clarification session (11:19 Descript clarification / VLD-MR1).
3. `TEAM_DECISION`: Strategic and architectural decisions established by Team Slave of Skynet.
4. `OBSERVED_EVIDENCE`: Sourced directly from manufacturer technical datasheets (TDS), public regulatory standards, or active catalogue listings.
5. `OBSERVED_IMPLEMENTATION`: Sourced directly from inspected, verified repository code and test suites.
6. `DERIVED_EXACT`: Deterministic arithmetic derivation from exact observed values (e.g. price per unit derived from box price).
7. `ESTIMATED`: Bounded engineering model with explicit assumptions (low, central, high). Never presented as a single point fact.
8. `ASSUMED`: Plausible operational hypothesis adopted for engineering evaluation; not sponsor-confirmed.
9. `UNKNOWN`: Fact is not established by evidence; preserved transparently as an open gap.
10. `CONFLICT`: Two competing values exist in official manufacturer documentation without mutual cancellation.

## 3. Claim Audit Invariants

To maintain machine-verifiable integrity, any generated text, script, or presentation must adhere to these five non-negotiable rules:

1. **No Single-Point Baselines:** Whenever `B1-ESTIMATED` (2.59–12.46 g, central 6.50 g) is cited, it must appear with its interval or the explicit qualifier *"modeled scenario"*. Profi's actual incumbent mass and cost remain `UNKNOWN`.
2. **Zero-Crossing Range Disclosure:** Any citation of C5 BIOPAP virgin plastic reduction MUST state the full range (`−67.6% to +82.1%`) and disclose that it crosses zero. Citing the central +52.5% alone is strictly prohibited.
3. **No Cross-Configuration Borrowing for C6:** C6-RO-P portion pricing (1.15–1.29 RON) must never be combined with C6-RO-H oven temperature (280°C) or C6-EU recycled content.
4. **Body vs System Thermal Distinction:** Temperature limits for C4 (220°C), C5 (175/185°C), and C6-RO-H (280°C) apply strictly to tray bodies. Transparent closures and heat-seal films are separately qualified components.
5. **No False Commercial Closures:** E-commerce catalogue listings (`ROMANIA_DISTRIBUTOR_CURRENT`) demonstrate commercial availability routes, not guaranteed warehouse stock, lead times, or volume contracts (`listing ≠ stock`).
