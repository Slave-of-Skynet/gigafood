# HTF-01 — Source ledger

**Project:** Biomentorhub × Profi sustainable high-temperature food packaging landscape<br>
**Research cut-off:** 2026-09-26<br>
**Status:** Evidence register supporting `HTF-01-systematic-landscape-report.md`<br>
**Rule:** a source can support only the claim written in its row. A polymer-family statement is not an SKU qualification; a body statement is not a closed-pack statement; recyclability by design is not proof of local collection/reprocessing.

## Evidence labels

| Label | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Primary** | Regulation, official authority, manufacturer TDS/DoC/certification or named product page. |
| **Secondary** | Trade press, distributor or retailer. Useful for discovery/price only unless corroborated. |
| **PASS-capable** | Can support the stated gate within its exact scope. |
| **Context only** | Cannot by itself qualify a complete packaging system. |
| **Adversarial** | Records a limitation, contrary fact or route exclusion. |

## Source-ledger conclusions

1. No source qualifies a **complete, transparent, closed, disposable retail pack at 250°C** with food-contact, six-hour holding, exact sustainability and Romania/Moldova recovery evidence.
2. The strongest commercial temperature evidence clusters at **210–225°C** for transparent flexible systems and **220°C** for CPET/board systems.
3. Aluminium bodies have credible evidence above 250°C, but ordinary clear PET lids are explicitly remove-before-oven; the window and closure break the complete-pack gate.
4. The only exact six-hour evidence found is conditional BIOPAP LC holding at **90°C**; it cannot be transferred to other BIOPAP lines or to 190–250°C exposure.
5. Every local-EOL conclusion remains construction- and operator-specific. National infrastructure and “recycle-ready” claims are context, not acceptance.
