## RFQ content, ready to use

Ask the listed manufacturer/seller to confirm the following for the exact nominated body, film/lid and closure. This packet prepares questions; it does not send them.

- Current article codes and compatibility drawing; usable fill volume/headspace; tray, film/lid, seal, coating and label net masses.
- Complete layer materials/gauges, plastic-bearing coatings and additives; measured recycled and renewable fractions with scope and certificate basis.
- DoC and migration dossier for the actual fatty/salty food, fill temperature and six-hour cabinet profile; maximum time/temperature separately for body and transparent component.
- Exact clear film/reel width, sealing equipment and conditions, anti-fog and leak/grease results. For BIOPAP, resolve 175 vs 185 C in writing.
- Stock for those articles, minimum trial order versus industrial MOQ, lead time and delivery to the named Romanian site.
- Unit prices at trial/production quantities, VAT basis, freight, Incoterms if relevant, sealer tooling/equipment and any separately priced conversion.
- Waste-operator acceptance/certification scope for the actual complete used pack, without generic recyclability assurances.
