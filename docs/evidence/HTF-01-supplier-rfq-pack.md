**Subject:** Technical qualification and commercial RFQ — high-temperature prepared-food packaging for Profi Romania/Moldova

Dear `[supplier]`,

Biomentorhub and Profi are evaluating lower-virgin-plastic and lower-impact retail packaging for prepared hot foods. We are seeking an **existing commercial product or a clearly identified custom development**, not a generic polymer recommendation.

# 9. Evaluation and no-go rules

## 9.1 Hard no-go

Reject or redesign when any of the following applies:

- exact complete-pack temperature/time is below the measured process;
- only melting point, OM7 or polymer-family data are offered;
- clear lid/window must remain in the oven but its exact grade is remove-before-oven;
- no current food-contact documentation covers the exact construction/intended use;
- migration, NIAS, PFAS, fibre/particle, leak or pressure result fails;
- six-hour hold is inferred from a different product line or lower duration;
- supplier will not disclose layer structure under NDA;
- “recyclable/compostable locally” has no named route and is central to selection;
- cost claim excludes a material operational component; or
- a pilot/R&D technology is represented as an available commercial SKU.

## 9.2 Comparative score only after hard-gate pass

For candidates that pass safety, exact temperature and pack integrity:

| Dimension | Weight | Evidence basis |
|---|---:|---|
| Full-pack virgin-plastic reduction | 20% | Exact BOM and certified PCR/renewable content |
| Practical RO/MD recovery/reuse | 20% | Named route, acceptance and scale |
| Total cost | 20% | Like-for-like landed/operating model |
| Retail/line performance | 15% | Stable line and store pilot |
| Food quality/waste | 10% | Yield, shelf life and sensory data |
| Carbon/resource result | 10% | Exact-BOM scenario LCA |
| Supply resilience | 5% | Capacity, lead time, dual source, change control |

A high weighted score cannot compensate for a hard-gate failure.
