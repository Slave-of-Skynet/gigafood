# ASTRA-R2 — Demo Business Cases

> **Epistemic note:** All figures are engineering estimates (ESTIMATED/ASSUMED). LOW/CENTRAL/HIGH are
> independent worst-/best-case corners of model inputs, not a statistical confidence interval. Annual
> volume assumptions dominate the annual-spend ranges. No value represents Profi's actual procurement
> price, volume, or margin. Do NOT present CENTRAL values as measurements.

### Business case narrative

In the central scenario, C1 costs approximately **1.05 RON more per pack** than the baseline estimate
(~+105%), which translates to ~**2.4 M RON/year** additional network cost. However, under the all-liner
accounting convention, C1 is classified as having **more** virgin plastic accountability than baseline
in the central scenario (−4.58 g net, i.e., C1 is worse). This is a construction artifact of counting
the full cellulose liner as "plastic" in the conservative budget — the renewable content (91.4%) is tracked
separately and independently.

The uncertainty interval spans from −4.4 M RON/year to +36.6 M RON/year incremental, meaning the
economic case is highly sensitive to paper/film converter pricing and import costs.

**Positive outcome requires:** Sacma B.Life Gaia with a confirmed hot-hold liner grade, supplier quote,
and a separate real plastic BOM replacing the accounting scenario.

**Baseline:** B1-ESTIMATED (modeled incumbent bag, used as incumbent comparator even for portions).  
**Candidate:** C5 BIOPAP LC SI-14 tray + compatible transparent film.

> **Thermal CONFLICT retained:** BIOPAP LC family has a documented conflict between the 2016 historical
> 175 °C ceiling and 2026 press claims of 185 °C. The six-hour 90 °C hot-hold claim requires a

### Business case narrative

In the central scenario, C5 avoids approximately **2.16 g/pack** of virgin plastic accountability
relative to B1-ESTIMATED, or **~13,700 kg/year** across the portions network. However, the
uncertainty interval spans negative avoidance at the LOW bound (−3.34 g, meaning C5 is worse if
the incumbent bag is lighter than central).

The cost premium is substantial: ~**1.95 RON/pack** (+270%), translating to ~**12.4 M RON/year** additional
network cost. At 901 RON/kg virgin avoided (central), the economics require strong sustainability
positioning or regulatory pressure to justify.

**Qualification blocker:** The thermal CONFLICT on the BIOPAP LC body and unidentified film must be
resolved through physical qualification before C5 can serve as a recommended candidate for hot-hold.

## 3. Literal 250 °C Fallback: B1-ESTIMATED → C6-RO-H

**Stream:** Whole chicken (P1), LITERAL_OVEN_250C_THEN_HOLD workflow.  
**Baseline:** B1-ESTIMATED.  
**Candidate:** C6-RO-H (E-ambalaj e-pui225 aluminium body + modeled post-oven clear lid).

> **Critical thermal limitation — NOT turned into PASS:**
> The E-ambalaj e-pui225 aluminium body is rated −40 to 280 °C by the seller (no duration specified).
> The **post-oven clear lid** is modeled as PET-like and has a screening ceiling of 60–70 °C —
> far below any oven exposure. The LITERAL_OVEN_250C_THEN_HOLD gate outcome is **BLOCKED**
> because the assembled system (body + lid) cannot be qualified without a lid that survives oven conditions.
> Economic and environmental arithmetic is provided for planning purposes only. This arithmetic
> does NOT constitute a route-to-market recommendation under the current lid architecture.

### Business case narrative

C6-RO-H adds approximately **21.6 g/pack** additional virgin plastic accountability relative to the
incumbent baseline (central), making it environmentally worse on this metric in every scenario.
The 20.8% secondary aluminium content is a moderate positive, but the unqualified lid negates it
for any plastic-reduction narrative.

The cost premium is ~**1.31 RON/pack** (+181%), or ~**4.1 M RON/year** additional.

**Path to unblocking:** Replace the post-oven lid with a material qualified to withstand oven insertion
(e.g., borosilicate glass, ovenable PET confirmed by supplier, or a silicon-based alternative). Any
such lid must independently qualify for the LITERAL_OVEN_250C_THEN_HOLD gate before the system
can receive a non-BLOCKED outcome.
